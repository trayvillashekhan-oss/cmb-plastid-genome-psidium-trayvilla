## Laboratory Questions and Answers

### 1. What is the scientific name of your chosen plant and its plastid genome accession number?

The chosen plant is *Psidium guajava* L. (guava), belonging to the family Myrtaceae. Its plastid genome accession number is **NC_033355.1**.

### 2. What is the total length of the plastid genome in base pairs?

The complete chloroplast genome of *Psidium guajava* is **158,841 base pairs (bp)**, as confirmed by the Galaxy sequence-length analysis.

### 3. Can you identify the LSC, SSC, IRa, and IRb regions on your map? Briefly describe their positions.

Yes. The **large single-copy (LSC)** region is shown along the upper portion of the circular genome map, while the **small single-copy (SSC)** region is located at the bottom. The two inverted-repeat regions, **IRa and IRb**, are positioned on opposite sides of the SSC region, separating it from the LSC region.

The published genome organization reports the following region sizes:

- LSC: 87,675 bp
- SSC: 18,464 bp
- IRa: 26,351 bp
- IRb: 26,351 bp
