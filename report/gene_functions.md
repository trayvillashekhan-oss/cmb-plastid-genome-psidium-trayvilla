# Gene Functions, Introns, and Pseudogenes

## Eight Protein-Coding Genes and Their Functions

The chloroplast genome of *Psidium guajava* contains genes involved in photosynthesis, gene expression, and protein synthesis. Eight examples of protein-coding genes and their functions are presented below.

| Gene | Function |
|---|---|
| psbA | Encodes the D1 protein of Photosystem II, which participates in photosynthetic electron transport. |
| psaA | Encodes a Photosystem I reaction center protein involved in light-dependent electron transport. |
| rbcL | Encodes the large subunit of Rubisco, an enzyme involved in carbon fixation. |
| atpB | Encodes a subunit of ATP synthase, which helps produce ATP. |
| petB | Encodes cytochrome b6, which participates in photosynthetic electron transport. |
| rpoB | Encodes a subunit of RNA polymerase involved in transcription. |
| rpl2 | Encodes a large ribosomal subunit protein involved in protein synthesis. |
| matK | Encodes a maturase protein involved in chloroplast RNA splicing. |

**Data source:** *Psidium guajava* chloroplast genome, NCBI accession NC_033355.1.
